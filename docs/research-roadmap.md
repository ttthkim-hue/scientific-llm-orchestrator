# Research roadmap

The research question is when independently verifiable bounded scientific
software tasks benefit from local or free hosted workers while authority stays
with a frontier reviewer.

Future work may compare frontier-only, hosted-worker, local-worker, and local-
then-hosted-residual settings on the same public fixtures. Primary outcomes
should include accepted functional quality, authority violations, hosted-token
displacement, latency, energy, fallback, and privacy suitability. No result or
paper outcome is promised by this prototype.

Research-credit applications, institutional commitments, provider forms,
promotion, and publication require exact text review and separate approval.
They are `not-submitted` here.
