# Code of conduct

Participants must be respectful, specific, and honest about evidence. Do not
harass, discriminate, impersonate, fabricate results, hide limitations, or
pressure contributors to publish private material or bypass an approval gate.

Project maintainers may pause or reject work that creates a privacy, security,
licensing, or research-integrity risk. Report concerns privately through the
project's approved maintainer channel; do not include sensitive material in a
public issue.
